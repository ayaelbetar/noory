# Purpose

Understand who we are designing for and ensure every product decision serves real user needs.

---

# Persona 1 — Primary User

## Name
Omar (Representative Child)

## Age
7 years old

## Background
- Learning to read Arabic.
- Loves stories.
- Uses a tablet with minimal assistance.

## Goals
- Read stories independently.
- Feel successful.
- Have fun while reading.

## Frustrations
- Afraid of making mistakes.
- Gets discouraged if corrected too much.
- Loses focus with complicated interfaces.

## Needs
- Encouragement.
- Simple interactions.
- Friendly guidance.
- Immediate support.

## Success Criteria

"I finished the story and I want to read another one."

## How Noor Helps

- Encourages every attempt.
- Offers narrator support **only after** the child’s first attempt on a page (**Retry** path), never before.
- Makes reading feel safe and enjoyable.

---

# Persona 2 — Secondary User

## Name
Parent

## Goals

- Encourage reading habits.
- Help the child practice independently.
- Trust the learning experience.

## Frustrations

- Doesn't always have time to listen.
- Wants positive educational screen time.

## Needs

- Child can practice safely.
- Child remains motivated.

## Success Criteria

"My child enjoys reading without needing me every minute."

## How Noor Helps

Acts as a supportive reading companion when parents are unavailable.

---

# Persona 3 — Future User

## Name
Teacher

## Status

Out of MVP

## Goals

- Monitor reading progress.
- Recommend practice.

## Future Needs

- Reading reports.
- Classroom insights.
- Performance trends.

---

# Persona Insights

## Emotional Need

Children need confidence more than correction.

## Functional Need

Simple, distraction-free reading practice.

## Business Need

Increase engagement and story completion inside Noory.

---

# PM Thinking

The product is intentionally optimized around the child's emotional journey. Parents benefit indirectly, while teacher tools are postponed until after the core reading experience is validated.

---

# Decision Summary

## Decisions Made

- Child is the primary persona.
- Parents are supporting users.
- Teachers remain outside MVP.
- Emotional needs take priority over technical accuracy.

## Open Questions

None.

## Future Enhancements

- Multiple age personas.
- Dyslexia-friendly persona.
- Special education adaptations.
- Parent personalization.
