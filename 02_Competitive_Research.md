# Competitive Research

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Final

---

# Objective

Study similar reading products and identify best practices that can improve the AI Reading Buddy experience while keeping Noory's identity.

---

# Products Reviewed

## 1. Noory

### Strengths
- Beautiful Arabic-first experience
- High-quality children's content
- Clean and friendly UI

### Opportunity
- Add interactive reading support.
- Increase engagement through AI companionship.

---

## 2. Google Read Along

### Strengths
- Real-time reading feedback
- Friendly reading companion
- Strong speech recognition

### Weaknesses
- Feels educational rather than magical.
- Feedback focuses more on correctness than emotional encouragement.

---

## 3. Duolingo ABC

### Strengths
- Excellent motivation
- Short interactions
- Fun learning experience

### Weaknesses
- Heavy gamification.
- Less focused on story-based reading.

---

## 4. Little Story

### Strengths
- Story-first experience
- Simple interface

### Weaknesses
- Limited intelligent interaction.
- No personalized reading companion.

---

# Best Practices

- Friendly companion instead of a strict evaluator.
- Positive reinforcement.
- Short encouraging messages.
- Clear reading progress.
- Minimal interface.
- Immediate feedback.

---

# Gaps We Identified

- AI often feels like a testing tool.
- Feedback can become repetitive.
- Limited emotional connection.
- Weak integration with the product identity.

---

# Our Product Decisions

## Noor Instead of Generic AI

Children interact with Noor, not with an AI assistant.

---

## Encouragement Before Accuracy

Building confidence is more important than highlighting mistakes.

---

## Narrator After Failed Attempts

Instead of repeating a bare **Retry** loop, Noor helps the child by playing the **narrator** audio.

---

## Child-First UX

Every interaction should reduce stress and increase confidence.

---

## Native Noory Experience

The feature should feel like it has always belonged inside Noory.

---

# Key Differentiators

- Noor as a permanent reading companion.
- Emotion-driven feedback.
- Positive language only.
- Reading feels like an adventure.
- Strong connection to the Noory brand.

---

# Final Takeaways

Technology is not the product.

The emotional experience is the product.

Children should remember Noor—not the AI.

---

# Decision Summary

## Decisions Made

- Friendly companion over AI assistant.
- Positive reinforcement over strict correction.
- Story-first experience.
- Minimal UI.
- Native Noory integration.

## Open Questions

None.
