# Vision

Help every Arabic-speaking child enjoy reading by transforming AI into a supportive reading companion rather than an evaluator.

---

# Product Summary

AI Reading Buddy introduces **Noor**, a friendly companion who listens while children read Arabic stories aloud.

Instead of correcting mistakes, Noor encourages effort, celebrates progress, and supports retries with narrator guidance.

---

# Target Audience

Primary:
- Arabic-speaking children (**3–8 years**) on the **Noory** learning platform

Secondary:
- Parents

Future:
- Teachers

---

# Core Principles

- Child First
- Confidence before correctness
- Arabic-first
- RTL experience
- Positive reinforcement
- AI remains invisible
- Simple UX
- Scalable architecture

---

# MVP Features

- Read with Noor
- Voice recording
- Arabic Speech-to-Text
- Page-level evaluation
- Positive feedback
- **Narrator** on **Retry**
- Decision 7: **Continue** after 3 **Retry** outcomes per page.
- **Reading Summary** (pages + effort; no child-facing score)

---

# Key Decisions

- Noor is a companion.
- Arabic-only MVP.
- Minimal UI.
- Modular architecture.
- Provider-independent AI.
