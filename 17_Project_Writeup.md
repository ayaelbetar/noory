# Executive Summary

**AI Reading Buddy** is an MVP feature for Noory's Arabic children's reading platform.

**Noor** listens while children read aloud, responds with **Success** or **Retry**, supports **narrator** playback, and closes with a **Reading Summary**—without exam-style scoring.

---

# The Problem

Children benefit from read-aloud practice; adults are not always available. Punitive tools can harm confidence.

---

# The Solution

**Reading Session** with Noor: encouragement, **Retry** path with **narrator**, Decision 7 **Continue** after 3 **Retry** outcomes, Arabic-first UX.

---

# Target Users

Primary: children **3–8** (Arabic) on **Noory**. Secondary: parents/caregivers.
