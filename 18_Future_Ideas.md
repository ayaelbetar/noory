# Feature ideas backlog (post-MVP)

## Engagement

- **Parent dashboard** — completed stories, reading frequency, milestones (not live proctoring)
- **Reading history** — continue unfinished stories; review completed books
- **Achievements** — effort-based badges (e.g. First Story, Brave Reader); no accuracy ranks

## Personalization

- Personalized encouragement and recommendations
- Adaptive reading difficulty (explicitly out of MVP per `03_Product_Decisions.md`)

## AI coaching (explicitly out of MVP)

- Word-level pronunciation feedback
- Fluency and pace insights
- Difficult-word detection

## Classroom (future)

- Teacher portal: classroom overview, assignments, aggregate progress — not child-facing ranks

# Success Criteria for Expansion

Only move beyond MVP after validating:
- Reading engagement
- Story completion
- Positive usability feedback
- Stable AI performance

---

# PM Thinking

Every future feature should solve a validated user problem. Features should not be added simply because they are technically possible.
